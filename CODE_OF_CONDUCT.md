# Code of conduct

AnyDrop is maintained by Vedra Tech and is open to everyone. To keep it a friendly place to ask, report and contribute:

- Be respectful and patient. People of every background and experience level are welcome.
- Discuss the code, not the person. Criticism should be specific and constructive.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- No spam or self-promotion unrelated to the project.
- Respect people's privacy: do not share anyone's private information.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainers privately through the **Security** tab ("Report a vulnerability" also reaches the maintainers privately), or mention it in a private message to `@vedratech` on GitHub.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who do not follow this code.
