M2: the upload engine. `AnyDropCore` now uploads: whole files or chunks (`Blob.slice()` only) with Dropzone's `dz*` fields plus `adRelativePath`/`adSessionId`, `parallelUploads` files at once, `parallelChunkUploads` (`boolean | number`, as in Dropzone 6.3.5) chunks per file, an optional `maxChunksInFlight` cap shared round-robin, retry with exponential backoff, jitter and `Retry-After` (`retryChunks` defaults to `true`), a lazily sent per-batch manifest (`sendManifest`, `manifestUrl`), finalize (`finalizeUrl`, or a `chunksUploaded(file, done, instance)` hook), `chunkStatusUrl` after a `409 CHUNK_MISMATCH`, `uploadMultiple`, `binaryBody`, `transformFile`, and a pluggable `transport` (built-in `xhrTransport`). New and newly emitted events: `processing`, `sending`, `uploadprogress`, `totaluploadprogress`, `folderprogress` (throttled), `success`, `complete`, `queuecomplete`, the `*multiple` variants, and `removedfolder`. New methods: `processFile(s)`, `uploadFile(s)`, `disable()`/`enable()`, `resolveOption()`. `on()`/`once()` return a chainable unsubscribe function. A string `method` is upper-cased; deprecated `renameFilename` and `acceptedMimeTypes` are mapped. New client error codes `UNREADABLE`, `TRANSFORM_FAILED`, `FINALIZE_FAILED`, and `INVALID_PATH` for paths with `..`.
